1. Executive Architecture Overview

Apna Campus (CollegeFlow) is an autonomous college ERP and institutional management system built for engineering institutions. It unifies 14 distinct roles into a coherent, role-based platform with strict security, continuous evaluation tracking, fee verification queues, and biometric gate pass controls.

4. Key Domain Subsystems & Business Workflows

A. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Security Architecture

Stateless authentication powered by Passport JWT. Handlers are decorated with @Roles(Role.FACULTY, Role.HOD) and intercepted by RolesGuard. Passwords use bcryptjs hashing.

B. Digital Fee Payment Proof & Accounts Verification

Students upload payment receipts (UPI/NEFT/Challan) with transaction reference numbers. Accounts officers review the queue at /api/accounts/payment-verifications, approve/reject with remarks, updating student ledgers and generating PDF fee receipts instantly.

C. Autonomous Examination & Grading Engine

Strict compliance with BPUT autonomous engineering standards. Handles continuous internal assessment (30 marks) and end-term exams (70 marks) with SGPA/CGPA computations, grade tables, backlog tracking, and barcode hall ticket printing.

D. Biometric Attendance & Dual-Step Hostel Gate Pass

Integrates real-time biometric entry/exit tracking. Students request passes which require Warden approval followed by Security Gate verification via dynamic QR code.

E. AI Campus Assistant (Apna AI Chatbot) & CampusConnect

Integrated campus AI assistant providing immediate guidance on exams, attendance policies, and fee dues, paired with CampusConnect feed and WhatsApp communication.
